Hiver SDE Assignment — Baseline Results

Dataset:
- Spotify customer-support interactions
- Human-labeled gold set: 200 examples

Baseline 1 — Majority Classifier:
- Majority intent: PLAYBACK_OR_APP_ISSUE
- Accuracy: 37.0%

Baseline 2 — TF-IDF + Logistic Regression:
- 3-fold stratified cross-validation
- Out-of-fold accuracy: 34.5%
- Macro F1: 0.06

Retrieval Sanity Check:
- Gold examples: 200
- Top-1 intent match: 25.0%
- Top-3 intent match: 51.5%

Interpretation:
- TF-IDF classification does not beat the majority baseline.
- TF-IDF retrieval can find related examples, but is not reliable enough
  to determine intent by itself.
- Retrieval will therefore be used as supporting context rather than as
  the sole decision mechanism.
